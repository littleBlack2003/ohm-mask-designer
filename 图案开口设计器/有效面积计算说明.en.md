# Opening Statistics and Per-Pixel Effective-Area Uncertainty

[English](有效面积计算说明.en.md) | [简体中文](有效面积计算说明.md) | [繁體中文](有效面积计算说明.zh-Hant.md)

The "Opening statistics / Active-region area" panel on the right counts single-cell pattern apertures, whole-panel pattern apertures, mounting holes on both pieces, and frame positioning openings. ITO reference regions, marking text, and merged pick notches are not counted as holes. Every pattern aperture must be tagged as active region, common electrode region, or untagged.

"Active-region area & uncertainty…" computes only openings tagged as active; common-electrode and untagged openings are excluded. Each active region is reported independently — areas and uncertainties are not summed. The effective area is defined as the intersection of that active-region aperture with the unioned ITO area; if ITO is not defined, the whole substrate is not assumed effective. After changing the design, role tags, or errors, previous results are invalidated and must be recomputed.

## Error input and propagation formula

The default pattern-size tolerance is ±0.01 mm and is adjustable. When the rectangular-distribution half-width is a, the input standard uncertainty is u(q)=a/√3. With "Standard uncertainty · 1σ" selected, enter u(q) directly with no conversion; switching modes does not auto-convert the value.

It uses the first-order error-propagation formula for independent inputs:

u(A)=√[Σ(∂A/∂qᵢ × u(qᵢ))²]; U=2u(A), k=2.

No random sampling is used; sampling means or quantile intervals are not reported. U=2u does not claim an exact 95% coverage. When the nominal area is 0, the relative uncertainty is undefined.

## Area and analytical sensitivity

- Fully contained rectangle: A=wh; ∂A/∂w=h, ∂A/∂h=w.
- Fully contained circle: A=πd²/4; ∂A/∂d=πd/2.
- Fully contained slot: A=w(l−w)+πw²/4; ∂A/∂l=w, ∂A/∂w=l+(π/2−2)w.
- Same-orientation rectangle cut (supports a common rotation and a 90° difference): in the ITO local frame, Lx=max(0, min(x_right, xITO_right) − max(x_left, xITO_left)), similarly for Ly, and A=Lx·Ly. Take the analytical derivative of the currently active min/max branch, then propagate via ∂A/∂q=Ly·∂Lx/∂q + Lx·∂Ly/∂q.

w/h are the aperture's full width/height, W/H are the ITO's full width/height, d is the diameter, and l is the slot's total length. The X/Y relative-positioning errors represent the translation between the aperture and the ITO; the formulas convert them into the ITO local frame. Input size errors are not single-sided errors. Inputs are mutually independent, and the rotation angle is fixed.

When boundaries coincide exactly, the area function is non-differentiable; the larger absolute value of the left/right derivatives is used as a conservative linear estimate and flagged in that pixel's notes. This estimate is not equal to the standard deviation of the full nonlinear area distribution. If a nonzero gap is smaller than the boundary movement the error may cause — so a cut branch could change — u is not reported, with a note that the first-order formula does not apply; when the standard uncertainty is entered directly, 3u is used for this local applicability check.

Complex cuts do not yet provide analytical uncertainty — only the nominal area and a note are shown. Same-orientation rectangle cuts and fully contained apertures with enough boundary margin are supported. Polygons have no width/height size-error model, so u is not reported when the corresponding size error is nonzero. Curved-intersection nominal areas use a high-density polyline approximation; full circles and slots use exact formulas.

## Scope and export

The ITO size and relative-positioning errors default to 0, meaning they are excluded — not that they are measured to be 0. Shared manufacturing size deviations, substrate errors, deformation, evaporation shadowing, and angular errors are not included.

The CSV contains opening counts, the error model, input parameters, and per-active-region nominal area, u, U, relative u, formula, and notes, without an area total. The parameters are saved with the browser draft, project draft, and design JSON, and do not change the evaporation-mask contour.
