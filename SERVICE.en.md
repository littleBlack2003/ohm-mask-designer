# Mask Fabrication Service

[English](SERVICE.en.md) | [简体中文](SERVICE.md) | [繁體中文](SERVICE.zh-Hant.md)

Wuhan Ohm Technology Co., Ltd. provides mask fabrication and manufacturing-file review services.

Customers who bring complete manufacturing drawings can get discounted quotes. Pricing depends on mask size, material, thickness, quantity, precision, and lead time. Please send your drawings and requirements to <dasohm@163.com>.

When requesting a quote, please provide:

- DXF, SVG, or other editable vector manufacturing drawings.
- Mask outline dimensions, material, and thickness.
- Quantity, precision, surface treatment, and lead-time requirements.
- Manufacturing instructions for the pattern, reference, and laser-marking layers.
